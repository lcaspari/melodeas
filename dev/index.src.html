    No tracks yet. Pick a sound below, press the red button, and sing or beatbox one round of the loop.

    In the selected track: drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to change its length, double-click it to remove it, double-click an empty spot to add one. Click a drum hit to switch drum. Recording again adds to what is there.

    Add a track

    Take it to Ableton

    The MIDI file has one track per instrument, the tempo, and every note's velocity. Drag it into Ableton's Session view and each track lands as its own clip. Drums use the General MIDI layout (kick C1, snare D1, closed hat F♯1, open hat A♯1), which is where most Ableton drum racks keep them. Voice tracks are audio, so they export as WAV from their own track.

    Timing offset, ms

    The timing offset is how long sound takes to leave the speakers and come back through the mic. If your notes land late or early against the click, measure it once: turn the speakers up and press “Measure with speakers”.
